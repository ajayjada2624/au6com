Step-by-Step Withdrawal Process

  1. Open AU6 App: Launch the app and log in to your account.
  2. Go to Wallet: Tap on the Wallet or Withdraw icon located in the main lobby.
  3. Add Your Payment Details: Select UPI and enter your correct UPI ID (e.g., yourmobile@ybl or yourname@okaxis). Verify the name matches your bank records.
  4. Enter Amount: Type the amount you wish to withdraw (minimum ₹100).
  5. Submit Request: Tap Confirm Withdrawal. Your funds will be credited within a few minutes.

Essential Rules for Smooth Payouts

  • Wagering Requirements: Ensure you have met any playthrough requirements linked to promotional deposit bonuses.
  • Accurate Details: Double-check your UPI ID before clicking confirm. Money sent to an incorrect ID cannot be reversed.
  • Bank Server Timing: UPI transactions are fastest between 9:00 AM and 11:00 PM. Payouts during late-night banking maintenance windows may take slightly longer.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum withdrawal amount on AU6?

The minimum withdrawal limit is ₹100 when cashing out via UPI or IMPS.

Why is my AU6 withdrawal pending?

Withdrawals usually process within 30 minutes. If your request is pending, it might be due to temporary UPI banking server downtime or security verification on your first cashout.

Can I withdraw money to someone else's UPI ID?

For account security and fraud prevention, it is strongly advised to withdraw money only to the UPI account matching your registered mobile number.
