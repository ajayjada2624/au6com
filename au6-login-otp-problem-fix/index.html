How to Fix AU6 App Login & OTP Problems

If you are not receiving the one-time password (OTP) or experiencing login failure when signing in to the AU6 App, follow these quick solutions.

Common Fixes for Missing OTPs

1. Check Spam & Blocked Messages

Many Android devices with Truecaller or Google Messages automatically divert automated SMS shortcodes into the Spam and Blocked folder. Check your SMS spam archive.

2. Wait for 60-Second Timer Before Resending

Repeatedly tapping "Resend OTP" flags the SMS gateway for rate limiting. Wait out the 60-second cooldown timer, check that your SIM has network bars, and request again.

3. Clear App Cache Data

Go to your phone's Settings → Apps → AU6 → Storage and tap Clear Cache. Restart your device to refresh communication tokens.

4. Verify Country Prefix

Ensure you enter your 10-digit Indian mobile number without typing an extra +91 or leading zero 0, as the system appends the country code automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I recover my account if I changed phone numbers?

Contact AU6 customer support at support@au6com.com with your recent transaction receipts or UPI IDs to request a registered number update.

Can I sign in using an email address?

No, accounts are tied directly to active Indian mobile phone numbers for security and UPI payouts.
